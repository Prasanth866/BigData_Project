Problem #2:
SparkSQL - Movie Ticket Booking
A movie booking system receives:
Booking_ID, Customer_ID, Movie, Theatre, Tickets, Amount
Example:
B101,C101,MovieA,Theatre1,2,400
B102,C102,MovieB,Theatre2,4,800
B103,C103,MovieA,Theatre1,3,600
Tasks
 Identify bookings containing more than two tickets.
 Detect duplicate Booking IDs using a Bloom Filter.
 Create a DataFrame containing unique bookings.
 Find the total number of tickets sold for each movie.
 Calculate total revenue for each movie.
 Use Spark SQL to find the highest-revenue movie.

Problem #3: RDF:
Museum Management
A museum wants to develop a semantic knowledge base containing information about artworks,
artists, galleries and art periods.
Every artist is a person. Artists create artworks. Artworks are displayed in galleries and belong to
historical art periods.

Consider the following information:
 Leonardo and VanGogh are artists.
 MonaLisa was created by Leonardo.
 StarryNight was created by VanGogh.
 MonaLisa is displayed in Gallery1.
 StarryNight is displayed in Gallery2.
 MonaLisa belongs to the Renaissance period.
 StarryNight belongs to the PostImpressionism period.
 Gallery1 and Gallery2 are museum galleries.
 Renaissance and PostImpressionism are art periods.
Questions
1. Identify suitable classes.
2. Establish suitable subclass relationships.
3. Derive appropriate properties from the given information.
4. Specify the domain and range of each property.
5. Identify the individuals.
6. Represent the scenario using RDF/RDFS triples.
7. Add suitable rdfs:label and rdfs:comment statements.
